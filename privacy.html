Privacy Policy for CryptoGuard

Last updated: March 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

CryptoGuard does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not track users, use cookies, or require account creation for the free version.

2. Premium Subscriptions

If you subscribe to CryptoGuard Premium ($4.99/month), payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your payment information. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.

3. Smart Contract Scanning

When you scan a smart contract address, the analysis is performed using publicly available blockchain data. We do not store your scan history on our servers in a way that identifies you personally.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect any data processed through our app. All communications are encrypted.

5. Third-Party Services

CryptoGuard uses the following third-party services:

  • Stripe - Payment processing for Premium subscriptions
  • Public blockchain APIs - To retrieve smart contract data for analysis

6. Children's Privacy

CryptoGuard is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy
